OFFENSE + DEFENSE / 5 DAYS

Offensive Security OT

Advanced · Hands-on

An intensive exploration of industrial attacks and their countermeasures. Work with ICS architecture, PLC internals and real protocols; use Python and C/C++ to investigate vulnerabilities and develop assessment tools.

WHO IT IS FOR

OT security professionals and engineers familiar with ICS, programming and basic penetration testing tools.

Enquire about this workshop ↗

What you will cover

  • ICS architecture: SCADA, PLC, HMI and their roles in a plant
  • PLC internals: memory, I/O, peripherals and communication
  • Cell-control and supervisory networks; connections to IT
  • Industrial protocols for local and distributed control
  • Device discovery and vulnerability research
  • OT penetration testing and exploitation in a controlled environment
  • Python3 and C/C++ tool development; analysis of denial-of-service and crash behavior
  • Protection, hardening and defensive countermeasures

Prerequisites

  • Basic understanding of ICS architecture
  • Fundamentals of Python3 programming; C/C++ may also be used
  • Familiarity with programming concepts and basic tools such as Wireshark, Burp Suite and a debugger
  • Willingness to experiment, investigate and learn
